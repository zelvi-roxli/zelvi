## ✨ Features

- 🎧 **YouTube + own MP3s + podcasts** in one player
- 🧠 **Recommendation engine** — deity-aware (Khatu Shyam, Radha-Krishna…), mood-aware (sad, romantic, party, Punjabi…), learns from your plays AND skips
- ⬇️ **Offline downloads** — songs play with no internet
- 🔒 **Background & lock-screen playback** with media controls
- 🎙️ **Zelvi AI voice assistant** — speak a song name, it plays
- 🔍 **Song recognition** (Shazam-style)
- 🚀 Fast: streams pre-resolved before you even tap
